# Disclaimer – test project only, never wear it

**OpenPatch is a research and engineering test project. It is NOT a medical device.**

- It is **not** approved, certified, cleared or validated by any regulator (EU MDR, FDA or anyone else).
- **No one should ever wear this pump on the body, connect it to a cannula in a person, or use it to deliver insulin or any other drug to a human or animal.**
- Every test described in this repository is a **bench test**: water or insulin (e.g. expired insulin) is delivered **into a vial, beaker or onto a precision balance on a table** — never into a person or animal, never through a cannula inserted in a body.
- Insulin delivery errors can kill: over-delivery causes severe hypoglycemia, under-delivery or interruption causes diabetic ketoacidosis. This design has none of the verification, risk management, software validation, biocompatibility testing or quality system a real insulin pump requires.
- The Trio/LoopKit integration exists to exercise the communication protocol against a **pod on the bench**, never to dose a person.
- Provided "as is", without any warranty, under the licenses stated in the repository. The authors accept no liability for any use.

If you need an insulin pump, talk to your diabetes care team and use an approved device.
