# 01 Clinical evaluation plan: structure (MDR Art. 61, Annex XIV Part A; MDCG 2020-1, MDCG 2020-5/-6; MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev. 4 as legacy guidance)

> Test project, never used on a person. **No clinical data exists or will be generated by this project. No protocol for human use is written here.** This file is the structure the sponsor/legal manufacturer fills. MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev. 4 is the older MDD-era guidance still widely used as a method reference; under MDR the legal basis is Art. 61 and Annex XIV, with MDCG guidance **[VERIFY current MDCG 2020-1/2020-13 status]**.

## 2. Stage 1: identification of clinical data

Sources (Annex XIV Part A): (a) clinical data of the device (none), (b) clinical data of equivalent devices (see `02-equivalence.md`), (c) published literature on similar devices and on the state of the art for insulin infusion, (d) clinical investigations, (e) post-market data. Literature search protocol (databases, terms, inclusion criteria) to be written by the manufacturer; none was run.

## 3. Stage 2: appraisal

Appraisal of each data set for suitability and contribution; weighting. Nothing to appraise today.

## 4. Stage 3: analysis

Demonstrate conformity with GSPRs 1, 8 and the clinical GSPRs; compare with the state of the art; identify gaps for PMCF. Notified body consultation: for class IIb active devices administering medicinal products the clinical evaluation consultation procedure (Art. 54) normally applies **[VERIFY exemptions]**.

## 5. Stage 4: report (CER)

Clinical evaluation report with sign-off by qualified evaluators (qualification criteria in MDCG 2020-13 / Annex XIV), updated at least annually for class IIb when PMCF data exist **[VERIFY]**, and linked to the PSUR.

## 6. FDA view

Clinical data is not automatically required for a 510(k) of an ACE pump; the need is decided with FDA (Pre-Sub). A significant-risk device study needs an IDE (21 CFR 812). Special controls require a human-factors study, not a clinical study (880.5730(b)).
