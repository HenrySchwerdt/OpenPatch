## Primary operating functions (identified from the design, hazard-related)

Neutral list of functions a user interface exists for, for the manufacturer's task analysis: pairing, pod setup, reservoir filling by draw mode, priming, docking and undocking, starting/suspending delivery, reading status and alerts, acknowledging alarms, pod swap (release/activate), charging, disposal. The tasks themselves (steps, on-body procedures) are to be written by the manufacturer.
