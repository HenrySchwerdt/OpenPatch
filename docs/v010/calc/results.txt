Reading: with firmware adversarial, every hazard needs at least one HARDWARE fault. The single points are: LATCH_Q stuck high, WDT/OC clear path open, PFET stuck on
(H1/H2), and DOCK_TERM / DIRREV_TERM stuck high (H3). Redundancy does not cover them (one AND chain), so they are covered by diagnostics = proof tests:
  - latch proof test (boot + every 24 h): pulse LATCH_CLR_N and read the latch/MOT_ALLOW read-back: must fall (catches LATCH_Q stuck high and a dead clear path).
  - proof test at boot and every 24 h: with VMOT sense ADC, try to power the motor with exactly ONE term de-asserted at a time (MCU_EN=0 / no ARM / undocked+forward) and read VMOT_SENSE and I_MOT: must stay 0.
  - watchdog proof test: skip one TPL5010 DONE on purpose and read SMA/MOT_ALLOW read-back low within 2.2 s (done in a safe state, motor idle).
  - OC proof test: pulse the reference (OC_REF test injection resistor) and check LATCH_Q drops.
  - PFET-stuck-on detection: VMOT_SENSE > 0.3 V while MOT_ALLOW=0 -> alarm + MCU holds STBY low + (v0.2: series fuse/second PFET).
