## Motor beside the tank / transverse options (analysis)
* Motor beside the tank with exactly two spur gears is impossible: the gear centre distance is limited to about 5.5 mm by the 6.7 mm cavity height (gear tip radius <= 3.3), the motor axis would lie inside the tank footprint.
* Worm + wheel: infeasible, the worm axis must be tangent to the wheel pitch circle, i.e. >= 4.2 mm above or below the rod axis (z > 8.5), and a self-locking worm (eta 0.3-0.4) gives about 3.3 N < 5 N.
* Miter / bevel pair 90 deg (built): 1:1, eta ~0.9, only height-neutral option. Module 0.3 z16 does NOT exist as a catalogue item (BUY-LIST §0); z12 was bought instead (fallback m0.4 z12, same pitch cone as the old CAD).
* **Miter pair as built (2026-11-20, BUY-LIST §0):** module 0.3, **z12 : z12**, brass, pitch d 3.6, tip OD 4.02 (drawing), cone distance 2.55, AliExpress 1005010269458834 (2 gears per lot; 2 mm bore = gear A, 1 mm bore = gear B). Still 1:1, so **U/step 0.00703 and firmware preset 0x26 (`nfp-608-26-45-cup-miter-1-1`) do NOT change** (only the comment in `firmware-rs/openpatch-bench/Cargo.toml` still says z16:z16). Distances from the mating axis: drawing front 0.96, back 4.22 (2 mm bore) / 3.32 (1 mm bore). **Gear A (motor) is faced to front 1.2 / back 3.4** (front: the d2 angle magnet has radius 1.0 > 0.96; back: keeps the motor, the lane-2 saddle and the E screw in place; 2.2 mm of D-shaft bore remain). **Gear B (rod) is used as delivered** (0.96 .. 3.32, hub 2.8), drilled 1.25 and tapped M1.6, screwed onto the rod. CAD envelope = 45 degree tip cone to the pitch circle, back cone, hub (`op_cup.gear_profile`); engaged face width 0.60 mm (of 0.84), Lewis tooth bending 23 MPa at the gearhead pull-out torque. `cup_check.gear_mesh` checks z = z, tip OD = m (z + 2 cos 45), apex alignment of both envelopes, face width, magnet rim to gear A (0.20), motor shaft end to gear B (0.14). The floor reliefs under the gears are gone (tip radius 2.01 leaves 1.34 mm to the floor).

## Thrust (19 x 5.5 class plunger, estimate, VERIFY on the bench)
NFP-608-26.45: 1.6 gf cm x 26.45 x 0.64 = 2.65 N mm at the gearhead; miter 1:1 eta 0.9 -> 2.39 N mm at the rod; M1.6 x 0.35 screw eta 0.2 -> **8.6 N** at the plunger. Needed (bore 20.5 x 5.5, 106 mm2): seal breakaway 2.0 N (bench 1.6 N scaled by perimeter 48.6/37.7) + 30 kPa occlusion 3.2 N = **5.2 N**; available 8.6 N = **1.65x** (>= 1.5x rule met, requirement >= 5 N met, SF 2 = 10.4 N not met). No bought miter pair with a ratio < 1 was found (m0.3 z12 / z12 only), so a 0.8 ratio (z16 : z20, +25 %) is not available; the margin has to come from the screw efficiency (eta 0.2 assumed for steel rod on the brass nut; a brass rod gives about 0.185, 8.0 N) or the motor torque.
Plunger aspect 20.1 / 5.3 = 3.7 (limit 4.5); lip seals sit on the long flat sides as before (the stadium ring contacts the whole perimeter, oversize 0.15); anti-tilt: cup body 12.6 mm (OP-100) / 22 mm (OP-200) on a 20.5 mm width (L/W 0.62 / 1.08, check >= 0.5) plus two rings 3.25 mm apart; add guide fins if the bench shows yaw jamming.
Battery (`mechanical/battery-selection.md`): **Ampul 301030**, 3.0 x 10 x 30 mm, 90 mAh listed (60 mAh realistic per the Liter table), integrated protection chip, two wires, 4.00 EUR, https://ampul.eu/en/battery/6782-li-pol-battery-90mah-37v-301030 (page read; shipping to DE and the real size to be measured). Days per charge at 3.4 mAh/day (0.15 mA) with 0.85 x 0.8 derating: **about 12 days (60 mAh) to 18 days (90 mAh)**, far above 48 h + reserve; width, not capacity, is the limit. Backup: Soldered 350824 (40 mAh, JST-PH, BerryBase.de, about 8 days, dimensions to measure). Lane 2 stack (z): floor 0.9, cell 3.0 + 0.2 swell allowance (z 0.9-4.1), USB-C receptacle 3.26 mm stacked ABOVE the front end of the cell (z 4.2-7.46, opening in the +Y long wall, lid inner 7.6), main board 0.8 + parts <= 1.3 above the rest of the cell (z 4.2-6.4); the receptacle is soldered to the board by the PCBA, the cell wires run to the board edge (a PCM end of the cell needs a 1.5 mm tail, inside the 31.5 mm envelope). Lane 2 is then as long as the cell, so OP-100 returns to the lane-1 length.

## Axial load path (no thrust sleeve)
Forward thrust (plunger pushed out, rod pulled toward the motor, up to the 8.6 N available): nut in the plunger -> rod thread -> **collar** (brass tube 2.5 x 0.45, ID 1.6, cut to 1.0, bonded on the rod) -> bush **rear** face (bearing annulus 2.64 mm2) -> bush -> housing.
Reverse thrust (plunger pulled back, assisted fill or retract; rod pushed away from the motor) = the **stop that used to be the sleeve**: nut -> rod thread -> **M1.6 thread in gear B** (drilled 1.25 + tapped, Loctite 648) -> **gear B hub back face** (hub d2.8, bearing annulus 3.89 mm2) -> bush **front** face -> bush -> housing. Axial play = 0.05 + 0.05 (`bush.gear_gap`, `collar.gap`). With a right-hand rod, forward drive turns gear B in the sense that tightens it toward the bush (check the firmware's forward direction on the bench); the compound holds the reverse sense.
The bush (brass tube 3 x 1.0 cut to 1.0 and drilled to 1.7, or an SLA print) is **bonded (CA) into the U rib bore**: the rib is only as thick as the bush, so without the bond nothing holds it axially. Bond shear at 8.6 N: 1.8 MPa on 4.7 mm2 (CA on brass / PA12 3-8 MPa), rib root bending 28 MPa (PA12 MJF ~ 45): marginal, a thicker rib costs length (VERIFY on the first print). The magnet carries no thrust. Thread strip in the 2.4 mm brass hub is hundreds of N against at most 10 N.
Gear B on the rod, assembly: screw gear B onto the -X end of the rod (hub toward the tip) until the rod end is flush with its front face, bond the magnet on that face, slide the bush over the tip, then the collar, bond the collar with the 0.05 gap to the bush.
