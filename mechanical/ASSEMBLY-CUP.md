# Cup pod: housing features and assembly (`op_cup_asm.py`, check in `cup_check.py`)

Tools: tweezers, 1.0 mm / 1.5 mm hex or small Phillips / slotted screwdriver, a 2 mm drift for pressing the needle hub, a 0.5 mm pick, a fine-tip soldering iron (motor + cell wires only, spec §1a exception 2026-11-20). The PCBA is bought assembled; no glue except where listed.
Renders: `out/v010-cup/OP-100_assembly_labeled.png` (exploded in assembly order), `OP-100_iso_lid_off.png`. Check: `check.py --variant v010 --drive cup` (also accepts `--assembly`): each part group drops in from above in the order below along a free vertical path (own x-y swept from the part bottom to the cavity ceiling, free of the housing and of earlier groups); round parts in U seats are tested from their axis up.

## Order
1. **Housing** (bottom shell, MJF PA12 or SLA): rim gasket face at z 5.0, 3 posts (d2.0, pilots d1.0 x 4 mm for M1.2 thread-forming screws) in three corners, two tank stop blocks at the dry end (datum x), front datum = dock pocket face, U-cradle rib for the bush (slot open to the top, bore 3.05), motor snap saddles (C-ring clips, see step 3) with FPC groove to the lane-2 boundary and a pin pocket in the floor, battery tray (0.4 mm floor recess), board supports with top z 5.3 = board underside (two d1.0 ledges at x 41.5 / y 24.3 and 34.1 east of the cell, one shelf on the +Y wall at x 8.0-9.2, y 34.9-35.7), two board-screw bosses d1.8 with d0.95 x 3.6 pilots (W at x 8.9 / y 21.5 between gearhead and tank, E in the side_b strip beside the dock at x 37.40 / y 20.0 in OP-100 = dock front face + 2.24), USB-C opening (x 18.8-28.2, z 4.0-7.4) + 0.4 mm gasket recess in the +Y wall, clip slot in the pocket end wall, dock pocket (bottom open, edges free for the 3.5 mm slide).
2. **Cartridge, pre-assembled on the bench:** brass DIN 934 M1.6 nut (AF 3.5, pocket AF 3.6) pressed into the plunger hex pocket, two seals dropped into the grooves; plunger pushed into the tank from the dry end up to the front wall; rod with gear B + magnet: screw gear B (m0.3 z12, 1 mm bore drilled 1.25 and tapped M1.6, retaining compound Loctite 648) onto the -X end of the rod, hub toward the tip, until the rod end is flush with its front face (no thrust sleeve), bond the d2 x 1 magnet on that front face (CA / UV); slide the bush (brass tube 3 x 1.0 drilled to 1.7) then the collar (brass tube 2.5 x 0.45) over the rod tip, bond the collar on the rod with 0.05 mm gap to the bush (hub back face to bush front face also 0.05); the bush is bonded (CA) into the U rib when the cartridge is dropped in; thread the rod tip into the nut by turning gear B with tweezers until the plunger is at the FULL position (rod ends 0.4 mm off the cup bottom). Septum disc into its underside pocket. **Drop the cartridge in:** tank on the floor between the stop blocks and the pocket face, bush into the U rib.
3. **Motor + gear A:** gear A (z12, 2 mm bore, faced to 1.2 / 3.4 mm from the mating axis) pressed on the motor shaft (retaining compound only if it creeps), motor pressed from above into the two snap saddles (the can d6.0 spreads the lips with a light push, a click confirms; to remove, lift with a pick at one end), gear A meshes with gear B as it seats (rock gently); the terminal block sits in the floor pocket, the FPC lies in the groove. Nothing from the lid touches the motor.
4. **Battery** (Ampul 301030) into the tray at x 9.37-40.87 (1.5 mm east of the motor-side end of the lane), **lead end WEST (toward the motor)**: the two wires go up through the wire channel x 7.5-9.4 between the motor can and the cell. VERIFY-ON-SAMPLE which end the real cell's leads exit; if they exit east, turn the cell so they still face west (the cell is symmetric in its tray) and do not route wires past the antenna end.
5. **PCBA** (board with USB-C mid-mount receptacle in a north-edge notch, piezo, side button SW1 at the north edge, TMAG tab, dock hall): (a) hold the board vertically beside the pod and **solder the motor (4 wires, JST plug cut off) and cell (2 wires) to the west-end top-side pads** (spec §1a exception), insulate the cell wires first and solder one cell wire at a time; (b) lower the board, wires folded into the wire channel x 7.5-9.4, until it rests on the two ledges, the west shelf and the two screw bosses (all at z 5.3); the USB-C receptacle slides into the wall opening; (c) **screw the board down from the top: 2 x M1.2 x 4 brass cheese-head screws** through the d1.4 board holes into the boss pilots (thread-forming; hand-tight, about 0.05 N m, do not strip the resin: max about 5 re-assemblies, then re-drill 1.1 and use M1.4). The board must be screwed down before the lid goes on.
6. **Gaskets and lid:** USB-C gasket frame into the wall recess (the lid's three hold-down ribs land on the board top at the ledge / shelf points, the button nub on the SW1 actuator), flat rim gasket (0.5 mm, 20 % squeeze) on the rim, lid on (roof rests on the three posts, gasket is squeezed 0.1 mm), 3 x M1.2 thread-forming screws from the TOP (flush countersunk heads in the clear lid, no washer under the head (seal each head with a dab of cast silicone)).
7. **Dock parts from BELOW** through the pocket window (pod on its back): flat silicone ring (2.5 / 3.3 x 0.5, cut from the 0.5 mm sheet) into the face groove around the hub seat; needle + hub pressed into the front wall seat with the drift; guard sleeve (spring) over the needle; U-clip (301 FH) pushed up into the pocket (bridge into the slot in the end wall). This is the only group that cannot be inserted from above: the pocket roof covers the needle path. After this the pod is closed.

## Features added in the housing pass (geometry only; no component or keep-out moved)
Exterior (user feedback): clear lid with a clean top (no vent, piezo port or button membrane: piezo and button sit on the board, reachable with the lid off), pebble shape: outer vertical corners R4.8, top edge fillet R1.8 (the largest the unchanged inner cavity allows: min wall 0.5 at the inner top corner; R2.8 would need the keep-outs 1 mm off the walls), flat bottom with a 0.3 mm edge break only. Lid closure: 3 posts in the bottom corners, M1.2 screws from the top with flush countersunk heads (d2.4 x 0.6) sealed by a dab of silicone over the head (no washer), flat gasket on the rim (lid starts 0.4 mm above the rim, total height unchanged 8.5); motor cradle: 2 snap saddles (C-ring lips in the bottom shell, nothing hangs from the lid); tank seat: 2 stop blocks + pocket face + O-ring groove (3.4 / 2.4) around the hub seat; bearing U rib; battery tray; board ledges; USB-C opening in bottom AND lid wall with gasket recess; clip slot; nothing else in the lid (only the gasket seat and the 3 corner screw interfaces).

## Cannot be done cleanly / open
* Dock parts (group 7) go in from below; the pocket roof blocks any other path. Needle press-in with the pod on its back needs a 2 mm drift and a support under the front wall.
* Screws are not drawn; only 3 posts fit (no free boss beside the full-width tank, +Y rear corner is taken by the motor): lid retention at the long walls relies on the roof seating on the posts and the gasket squeeze, check lid flex on the first print. A snap rim would need wall thickening.
* Motor and cell are soldered to west-end board pads (spec §1a exception 2026-11-20); the wires are not modelled, only their channel x 7.5-9.4 (no bottom-side parts above it). The TMAG strip path between the gearhead and the tank is not modelled either; the west screw boss (d1.8 at x 8.9, y 21.5) leaves 0.3-0.5 mm on each side for it.
* Side button: the flex window in the +Y lid wall (x 32.4-34.5, z 5.6-6.7, 0.45 mm resin left, 0.3 mm inner nub at x 32.6-33.4, z 6.15-6.65, face y 35.4; centred on the SW1 actuator at x 33.0) is **probably too stiff to work** (clamped 0.45 mm plate over a 1.1 mm tall window: a few um at 2 N). VERIFY on the first SLA print; fallback is a d1.2 through-hole with a pressed-in silicone key plug (flange inside) at the same position. The window keeps its offsets -0.6 / +1.5 mm from the actuator and stays clear of the USB-C gasket recess on the inner face (ends at x 29.1).
* Board hold-down ribs and supports are nominally zero-gap (lid inner 7.6 - board top 6.1 = 1.5 rib); a printed 0.05 mm short rib leaves the board held only by the two screws, which is acceptable.
* Gear A engages gear B while the motor drops (miter pair meshes tangentially): needs a small wiggle, the drop test excludes this pair.
* Wall minima of the new features: posts 0.5, hub seal groove wall 0.49, USB recess wall 0.5, FPC groove floor 0.35, gasket 0.5 wide (all >= 0.35; floor groove below the 0.4 hard floor is a documented exception).

## Sealing (bench tests run with the lid open; no other waterproofing features)
The only seal is the continuous **silicone gasket** between the shells (die-cut ring 0.5 mm thick x 0.4 mm wide, platinum-cure silicone, Shore A 40-50 (the sheet found is Shore A 60: more screw force, check lid flex), or a cut-to-size 0.6 mm cord), 0.5 mm free height squeezed to 0.4 mm by the lid roof resting on the three posts = **20 %** compression (target 20-25 %). The wall is only 0.9 mm thick, so a gasket groove is not possible without moving the keep-outs: the gasket sits flat on the rim 0.15 mm in from the outer edge. The three screws sit inside the gasket line (no room outside it), so they are sealed with a dab of cast silicone (BUY-LIST Z4) or CA over the flush head instead of the former 0.3 mm washer (no 0.3 mm sheet exists, and a 0.5 mm washer does not fit the 0.9 mm lid roof, see V010-CUP.md parts adaptation); the pilots are blind. The USB-C opening keeps its own 0.4 mm gasket frame and breaks the rim gasket there.

## Motor snap saddles (`op_cup.py`, params `cup.motor.snap_h / snap_t`)
Each of the two saddles (1.5 mm long, y = gearhead_y0 + 0.5 and motor_y1 - 2.2) is a C-ring: shell of radius 3.05 + t around the can axis, rooted in the floor only at its bottom (open relief to both sides, so the ring arcs flex), cut flat 1.6 mm above the can centre line. Positions, keep-outs and the envelope are unchanged; the +X side of the lane-2 saddle is limited by the battery keep-out and the -X side by the lid wall.
* Lip thickness t: 0.52 mm (lane-1 saddle, both sides); 0.32 mm on the +X side of the lane-2 saddle (outer radius 3.37, x <= 7.84; below the 0.5 mm MJF floor, so check on the first print or print that part in SLA).
* Lane-2 saddle +X lip cut at **z 5.2** (2026-11-20, board review fix 4) so it stays below the main board (underside z 5.3, west edge x >= 7.8; the lip ends at x 7.71). The can is still held: wrap at the bore 180 + 31.6 (-X lip, z 5.85) + 18.1 (+X lip, z 5.2) = **230 degrees**, mouth between the lip tips 5.53 mm < can d6.0 (0.47 mm snap instead of 0.81); retention of that saddle drops to roughly 60 % of the lane-1 saddle, the lane-1 saddle is unchanged.
* Undercut: the ring arc above the centre line closes to a mouth of 2 x sqrt(3.05^2 - 1.6^2) = 5.2 mm vs can d6.0, so each lip opens about 0.4 mm during snap-in; engagement angle about 32 degrees above the equator.
* Strain (estimate, curved cantilever, arc about 7 mm from root to tip, E 1.7 GPa PA12): 3 t d / (2 s^2) = 0.4 % (t 0.52, d 0.4), 1.2 % with a x3 margin for the ring curvature; allowable 3 % (PA12_MJF), 2 % (SLA tough).
* Retention: stiffness about 0.3 to 0.5 N/mm per lip, spread force about 0.2 N per lip, vertical pull-out about 0.15 to 0.2 N per lip (normal x tan 32 degrees + friction 0.3), so about 0.6 to 0.8 N for 4 lips against about 6 mN motor weight: it stays put, a pick lifts it out. Nominal lip clearance to the can 0.05 mm (held by geometry, no preload). `cup_check.py` tests the motor drop only down to the lip tips (the last 1.6 mm is the press-through).
