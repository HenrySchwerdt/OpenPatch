# Cup pod: housing features and assembly (`op_cup_asm.py`, check in `cup_check.py`)

Tools: tweezers, 1.0 mm / 1.5 mm hex or small Phillips screwdriver, a 2 mm drift for pressing the needle hub, a 0.5 mm pick. No soldering (the PCBA is bought assembled), no glue except where listed.
Renders: `out/v010-cup/OP-100_assembly_labeled.png` (exploded in assembly order), `OP-100_iso_lid_off.png`. Check: `check.py --variant v010 --drive cup` (also accepts `--assembly`): each part group drops in from above in the order below along a free vertical path (own x-y swept from the part bottom to the cavity ceiling, free of the housing and of earlier groups); round parts in U seats are tested from their axis up.

## Order
1. **Housing** (bottom shell, MJF PA12 or SLA): rim gasket face at z 5.0, 3 posts (d2.0, pilots d1.0 x 4 mm for M1.2 thread-forming screws) in three corners, two tank stop blocks at the dry end (datum x), front datum = dock pocket face, U-cradle rib for the bush (slot open to the top, bore 3.05), motor saddles (U, open top) with FPC groove to the lane-2 boundary and a pin pocket in the floor, battery tray (0.4 mm floor recess), two board ledges (z 4.15) beyond the cell, USB-C opening + 0.4 mm gasket recess in the +Y wall, clip slot in the pocket end wall, dock pocket (bottom open, edges free for the 3.5 mm slide).
2. **Cartridge, pre-assembled on the bench:** brass nut pressed into the plunger hex pocket, two seals dropped into the grooves; plunger pushed into the tank from the dry end up to the front wall; rod with sleeve + gear B + magnet (gear B on the sleeve, magnet on gear B's rear face: the only glue, cyanoacrylate / UV); slide bush then collar over the rod tip (collar pressed/bonded on the rod, 0.05 mm gap to the bush); thread the rod tip into the nut by turning gear B with tweezers until the plunger is at the FULL position (rod ends 0.4 mm off the cup bottom). Septum disc into its underside pocket. **Drop the cartridge in:** tank on the floor between the stop blocks and the pocket face, bush into the U rib.
3. **Motor + gear A:** gear A pressed on the motor shaft (retaining compound only if it creeps), motor dropped into the two saddles, gear A meshes with gear B as it seats (rock gently); the terminal block sits in the floor pocket, the FPC lies in the groove. The lid posts press the can (0.05 mm gap).
4. **Battery** (Ampul 301030) into the tray, wires toward the board edge; PCM end toward the motor side.
5. **PCBA** (board with USB-C receptacle, piezo, button, TMAG tab, dock hall tab, 4 motor + 2 cell spring contacts): lower onto the ledges / contact landings; USB-C passes into the wall opening (the receptacle is soldered to the board by the PCBA house). Board outline and keep-outs are those of the electronics agent; the ledges are under the aux zone only.
6. **Gaskets and lid:** USB-C gasket frame into the wall recess, flat rim gasket (0.5 mm, 20 % squeeze) on the rim, lid on (roof rests on the three posts, gasket is squeezed 0.1 mm), 3 x M1.2 thread-forming screws.
7. **Dock parts from BELOW** through the pocket window (pod on its back): O-ring into the face groove around the hub seat; needle + hub pressed into the front wall seat with the drift; guard sleeve (spring) over the needle; U-clip (301 FH) pushed up into the pocket (bridge into the slot in the end wall). This is the only group that cannot be inserted from above: the pocket roof covers the needle path. After this the pod is closed.

## Features added in the housing pass (geometry only; no component or keep-out moved)
Lid closure: 3 posts + flat gasket (lid starts 0.4 mm above the rim, total height unchanged 8.5); motor cradle: U saddles + lid posts; tank seat: 2 stop blocks + pocket face + O-ring groove (3.4 / 2.4) around the hub seat; bearing U rib; battery tray; board ledges; USB-C opening in bottom AND lid wall with gasket recess; clip slot; piezo port (d0.8, 0.2 recess) and button membrane (0.5 mm) in the lid roof over the aux zone.

## Cannot be done cleanly / open
* Dock parts (group 7) go in from below; the pocket roof blocks any other path. Needle press-in with the pod on its back needs a 2 mm drift and a support under the front wall.
* Screws are not drawn; only 3 posts fit (no free boss beside the full-width tank, +Y rear corner is taken by the motor): lid retention at the long walls relies on the roof seating on the posts and the gasket squeeze, check lid flex on the first print. A snap rim would need wall thickening.
* Spring contacts (motor 4, cell 2) and the FPC pad positions are defined by the electronics agent's board, the housing only provides the FPC groove and the ledges; the landing shelves are not modelled.
* Cell wires: the 301030 PCM end needs a 1.5 mm tail and a wire slot at the board edge, not modelled (inside the 31.5 mm envelope).
* Gear A engages gear B while the motor drops (miter pair meshes tangentially): needs a small wiggle, the drop test excludes this pair.
* Wall minima of the new features: posts 0.5, hub O-ring groove wall 0.49, USB recess wall 0.5, FPC groove floor 0.35, gasket 0.5 wide (all >= 0.35; floor groove below the 0.4 hard floor is a documented exception).
