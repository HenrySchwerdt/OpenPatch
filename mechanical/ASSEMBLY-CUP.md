# Cup pod: housing features and assembly (`op_cup_asm.py`, check in `cup_check.py`)

Tools: tweezers, 1.0 mm / 1.5 mm hex or small Phillips screwdriver, a 2 mm drift for pressing the needle hub, a 0.5 mm pick. No soldering (the PCBA is bought assembled), no glue except where listed.
Renders: `out/v010-cup/OP-100_assembly_labeled.png` (exploded in assembly order), `OP-100_iso_lid_off.png`. Check: `check.py --variant v010 --drive cup` (also accepts `--assembly`): each part group drops in from above in the order below along a free vertical path (own x-y swept from the part bottom to the cavity ceiling, free of the housing and of earlier groups); round parts in U seats are tested from their axis up.

## Order
1. **Housing** (bottom shell, MJF PA12 or SLA): rim gasket face at z 5.0, 3 posts (d2.0, pilots d1.0 x 4 mm for M1.2 thread-forming screws) in three corners, two tank stop blocks at the dry end (datum x), front datum = dock pocket face, U-cradle rib for the bush (slot open to the top, bore 3.05), motor snap saddles (C-ring clips, see step 3) with FPC groove to the lane-2 boundary and a pin pocket in the floor, battery tray (0.4 mm floor recess), two board ledges (z 4.15) beyond the cell, USB-C opening + 0.4 mm gasket recess in the +Y wall, clip slot in the pocket end wall, dock pocket (bottom open, edges free for the 3.5 mm slide).
2. **Cartridge, pre-assembled on the bench:** brass nut pressed into the plunger hex pocket, two seals dropped into the grooves; plunger pushed into the tank from the dry end up to the front wall; rod with sleeve + gear B + magnet (gear B on the sleeve, magnet on gear B's rear face: the only glue, cyanoacrylate / UV); slide bush then collar over the rod tip (collar pressed/bonded on the rod, 0.05 mm gap to the bush); thread the rod tip into the nut by turning gear B with tweezers until the plunger is at the FULL position (rod ends 0.4 mm off the cup bottom). Septum disc into its underside pocket. **Drop the cartridge in:** tank on the floor between the stop blocks and the pocket face, bush into the U rib.
3. **Motor + gear A:** gear A pressed on the motor shaft (retaining compound only if it creeps), motor pressed from above into the two snap saddles (the can d6.0 spreads the lips with a light push, a click confirms; to remove, lift with a pick at one end), gear A meshes with gear B as it seats (rock gently); the terminal block sits in the floor pocket, the FPC lies in the groove. Nothing from the lid touches the motor.
4. **Battery** (Ampul 301030) into the tray, wires toward the board edge; PCM end toward the motor side.
5. **PCBA** (board with USB-C receptacle, piezo, button, TMAG tab, dock hall tab, 4 motor + 2 cell spring contacts): lower onto the ledges / contact landings; USB-C passes into the wall opening (the receptacle is soldered to the board by the PCBA house). Board outline and keep-outs are those of the electronics agent; the ledges are under the aux zone only.
6. **Gaskets and lid:** USB-C gasket frame into the wall recess, flat rim gasket (0.5 mm, 20 % squeeze) on the rim, lid on (roof rests on the three posts, gasket is squeezed 0.1 mm), 3 x M1.2 thread-forming screws from the TOP (flush countersunk heads in the clear lid, 0.3 mm silicone washer under each head).
7. **Dock parts from BELOW** through the pocket window (pod on its back): O-ring into the face groove around the hub seat; needle + hub pressed into the front wall seat with the drift; guard sleeve (spring) over the needle; U-clip (301 FH) pushed up into the pocket (bridge into the slot in the end wall). This is the only group that cannot be inserted from above: the pocket roof covers the needle path. After this the pod is closed.

## Features added in the housing pass (geometry only; no component or keep-out moved)
Exterior (user feedback): clear lid with a clean top (no vent, piezo port or button membrane: piezo and button sit on the board, reachable with the lid off), pebble shape: outer vertical corners R4.8, top edge fillet R1.8 (the largest the unchanged inner cavity allows: min wall 0.5 at the inner top corner; R2.8 would need the keep-outs 1 mm off the walls), flat bottom with a 0.3 mm edge break only. Lid closure: 3 posts in the bottom corners, M1.2 screws from the top with flush countersunk heads (d2.4 x 0.6) sealed by a silicone washer under the head, flat gasket on the rim (lid starts 0.4 mm above the rim, total height unchanged 8.5); motor cradle: 2 snap saddles (C-ring lips in the bottom shell, nothing hangs from the lid); tank seat: 2 stop blocks + pocket face + O-ring groove (3.4 / 2.4) around the hub seat; bearing U rib; battery tray; board ledges; USB-C opening in bottom AND lid wall with gasket recess; clip slot; nothing else in the lid (only the gasket seat and the 3 corner screw interfaces).

## Cannot be done cleanly / open
* Dock parts (group 7) go in from below; the pocket roof blocks any other path. Needle press-in with the pod on its back needs a 2 mm drift and a support under the front wall.
* Screws are not drawn; only 3 posts fit (no free boss beside the full-width tank, +Y rear corner is taken by the motor): lid retention at the long walls relies on the roof seating on the posts and the gasket squeeze, check lid flex on the first print. A snap rim would need wall thickening.
* Spring contacts (motor 4, cell 2) and the FPC pad positions are defined by the electronics agent's board, the housing only provides the FPC groove and the ledges; the landing shelves are not modelled.
* Cell wires: the 301030 PCM end needs a 1.5 mm tail and a wire slot at the board edge, not modelled (inside the 31.5 mm envelope).
* Gear A engages gear B while the motor drops (miter pair meshes tangentially): needs a small wiggle, the drop test excludes this pair.
* Wall minima of the new features: posts 0.5, hub O-ring groove wall 0.49, USB recess wall 0.5, FPC groove floor 0.35, gasket 0.5 wide (all >= 0.35; floor groove below the 0.4 hard floor is a documented exception).

## Sealing (bench tests run with the lid open; no other waterproofing features)
The only seal is the continuous **silicone gasket** between the shells (die-cut ring 0.5 mm thick x 0.4 mm wide, platinum-cure silicone, Shore A 40-50, or a cut-to-size 0.6 mm cord), 0.5 mm free height squeezed to 0.4 mm by the lid roof resting on the three posts = **20 %** compression (target 20-25 %). The wall is only 0.9 mm thick, so a gasket groove is not possible without moving the keep-outs: the gasket sits flat on the rim 0.15 mm in from the outer edge. The three screws sit inside the gasket line (no room outside it), so they are sealed under the head with a die-cut 0.3 mm silicone washer (d2.4 / d1.3) in the countersink; the pilots are blind. The USB-C opening keeps its own 0.4 mm gasket frame and breaks the rim gasket there.

## Motor snap saddles (`op_cup.py`, params `cup.motor.snap_h / snap_t`)
Each of the two saddles (1.5 mm long, y = gearhead_y0 + 0.5 and motor_y1 - 2.2) is a C-ring: shell of radius 3.05 + t around the can axis, rooted in the floor only at its bottom (open relief to both sides, so the ring arcs flex), cut flat 1.6 mm above the can centre line. Positions, keep-outs and the envelope are unchanged; the +X side of the lane-2 saddle is limited by the battery keep-out and the -X side by the lid wall.
* Lip thickness t: 0.52 mm (lane-1 saddle, both sides); 0.32 mm on the +X side of the lane-2 saddle (battery keep-out at x0; below the 0.5 mm MJF floor, so check on the first print or print that part in SLA).
* Undercut: the ring arc above the centre line closes to a mouth of 2 x sqrt(3.05^2 - 1.6^2) = 5.2 mm vs can d6.0, so each lip opens about 0.4 mm during snap-in; engagement angle about 32 degrees above the equator.
* Strain (estimate, curved cantilever, arc about 7 mm from root to tip, E 1.7 GPa PA12): 3 t d / (2 s^2) = 0.4 % (t 0.52, d 0.4), 1.2 % with a x3 margin for the ring curvature; allowable 3 % (PA12_MJF), 2 % (SLA tough).
* Retention: stiffness about 0.3 to 0.5 N/mm per lip, spread force about 0.2 N per lip, vertical pull-out about 0.15 to 0.2 N per lip (normal x tan 32 degrees + friction 0.3), so about 0.6 to 0.8 N for 4 lips against about 6 mN motor weight: it stays put, a pick lifts it out. Nominal lip clearance to the can 0.05 mm (held by geometry, no preload). `cup_check.py` tests the motor drop only down to the lip tips (the last 1.6 mm is the press-through).
