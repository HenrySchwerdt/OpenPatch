# OpenPatch - mechanics (parametric CAD, Rev A "orderable")

EXPERIMENTAL DIY device, **not a medical device**. Rev A is for bench / water tests. Source of all dimensions:
`../spec/design-parameters.md` plus the Rev A decisions below (`params.json` is the single parameter source).

CAD kernel: SolidCraft command scripts (`solidcraft-cli`), generated by Python 3 (standard library only).

## Files

| file | purpose |
|---|---|
| `params.json` | the only parameter set (dimensions, drive presets, cannula placeholders A..N, contacts, mould, ...) |
| `op_layout.py` | computes all positions from the parameters (a size only changes `stroke`); steps -> U |
| `op_shell.py` | bottom shell, lid, gasket (closure, screw domes, cannula pocket + latch, motor saddles, bearing blocks, rail) |
| `op_drive.py` | reservoir, plunger core + lip seals, carriage + nut, gears, screw, bushings, magnet |
| `op_elec.py` | battery, PCB (+keep-out volume), coil, RF module, piezo envelope, switch, hall sensors, septum, needle, hub |
| `op_mould.py` | 2-part silicone mould for the plunger lip seals (size independent) |
| `op_build.py`, `op_cmds.py` | script assembly, part lists, interference exclude list, SolidCraft command emitter |
| `generate.py` | driver: scripts -> `.sc`, STEP, STL, PNG, `layout.json` |
| `check.py` | dimension / interference / wall / contact / RF-keep-out checks (exit 1 on failure) |
| `fab.py` | builds `out/fab/` (upload folder, per-file ordering sheet, cost estimate) |
| `parts.md` | purchased parts, prices, U/step recomputation, battery envelope, how reservoir/plunger/seal are made |
| `ASSEMBLY.md` | assembly sequence (no soldering iron) |
| `cannula-measurement.md` | measurement sheet for the Insight Flex connector plate (TODO-MEASURE values) |

## Regenerate

```bash
cd mechanical
python3 generate.py                          # OP-70/100/200 -> out/   (~5 min, 2 x 30 bodies + snapshots)
python3 generate.py --sizes OP-100 --no-snapshots
python3 generate.py --preset faulhaber_adm0620_16      # premium drive preset (see params.json drive_presets)
python3 generate.py --set battery.y=10 --set reservoir.fill_fraction=0.5
python3 check.py                             # all sizes in out/
python3 fab.py                               # out/fab/ for JLC3DP / PCBWay
```

SolidCraft must be installed at `/Applications/SolidCraft.app` (override with `SOLIDCRAFT_CLI`).
Open `out/OP-xx.sc` in SolidCraft for the feature history; sketches use fixed numbers, so change dimensions in
`params.json` and regenerate.

## What Rev A models (all three sizes, all bodies are separate solids)

* **Drive:** Zhaowei 6 mm planetary-gear stepper (cheap default, preset `zw_pd6_1stage`, 23.7 mm) or Faulhaber
  ADM0620 + 06/1 (premium preset). 1:1 spur pair (module 0.3, z = 18, centre distance 5.4) from the gearbox to an
  **M1.6 x 0.35 leadscrew** in its own lane beside the reservoir. Motor in saddles (3 per motor), screw in two
  bearing blocks with 3.0 mm bushings, **carriage** (nut hex pocket for DIN 934 M1.6, guide groove on a **rail** in
  the floor, tongue through the reservoir slot into the plunger socket), dia 2 x 1 **diametric magnet** on the screw
  end, hall sensors on the PCB underside facing the magnet (0.9 mm gap).
* **Fluid:** stadium reservoir 16 x 5 (open dry end), plunger core with two cast silicone lip rings (mould exported),
  fill septum (dia 4 x 2) with port, outlet channel dia 0.7 at z = 4.3, **30G needle in a press-fit hub**
  (dia 2.0 x 0.7 seat; UV adhesive only as fallback), cannula pocket fully parameterised from
  `cannula-measurement.md` (placeholders A..N, TODO-MEASURE) with wing grooves and a **snap latch** (wedge tooth +
  two relief slits per side).
* **Closure / sealing:** lap closure (lid skirt 0.45 over a 0.4 mm tongue) with a **0.5 mm silicone gasket**
  compressed to 0.45 mm, 4 x M1.2 x 6 screws with hard-stop domes (3 at the dry end, 1 near the coil), lid screw
  bosses; **button** = 0.5 mm membrane (dia 5) over a tactile switch (post dia 1.8); **piezo port** dia 1.2 with a
  dia 4.0 x 0.2 recess for an acoustic membrane (ePTFE/PU film disc on the inside of the lid).
* **PCB mounting / contacts (no soldering):** PCB rests on the tower top and two dia 1.0 locating pins, battery
  pocket with rib and tab channel, **motor shelf** (z = 3.9) for the motor FPC pads, **two lid hold-down pads** that
  preload the PCB onto SMD spring contacts (free height <= 1.2 mm; compress 0.6 mm onto the battery tabs, 0.9 mm
  onto the FPC pads). Contact positions are in `layout.json` -> `contacts`.
* **RF:** ISP1807 8 x 8 x 1 module in the +X/+Y PCB corner, antenna end facing +Y (outward); `rf_module.keepout_xy`
  is checked: no motor, screw, magnet, battery, coil or gear body intrudes (spring contacts/FPC pads at y = 25.7 are
  4.0 mm from the antenna end, battery edge 5.3 mm).
* **Qi coil:** dia 17.6 x 0.6 at z = 6.9 ... 7.5 (0.1 mm under the ceiling), four lid tabs hold it laterally.
  Top-side component height under the coil is therefore **1.4 mm** (was 1.05 mm).

`layout.json` also holds the envelope numbers (`envelope`), the contact positions, hall positions, PCB cut-outs,
RF keep-out, buzzer/button positions. Walls < 0.6 mm are listed as documented exceptions by `check.py`
(lid skirt 0.45, tongue/gasket 0.40, button membrane 0.5, septum boss 0.45, carriage side walls 0.55 -

`section_y_reservoir`, `section_y_screw`, `section_y_motor`, `section_plan_z4.2`. The upload folder is **`out/fab/`**.

## Known conflicts with the spec / decisions that need you (not silently changed)

1. **Battery:** with the 23.7 mm geared motor, its terminal shelf and the RF keep-out, the usable cell is only
   8.6 / 12.6 / 13.2 mm x 11.2 x 3.2 (OP-70/100/200) = ~17 / 25 / 26 mAh. **The >= 40 mAh target is not met**;
   see `parts.md` section 4 for the length needed (+10.9 / +6.9 / +6.3 mm) and alternatives.
2. **Piezo:** the 9 x 9 x 2 mm piezo does not fit next to the RF module, the button and the coil: envelope left
   is 1.6 / 5.6 / 6.2 mm x 2.6 mm (OP-70 effectively none). Use a smaller piezo, a bare disc glued to the lid, or a
   vibration motor in Rev B, or give up the button strip.
3. **Gear ratio / U per step:** the cheap drive is 5:1 (not 10:1): 0.0261 U per full step, pulses of 2 full steps =
   0.0522 U (spec 0.0131 U/step, 4 steps). Premium Faulhaber 16:1 = 0.00816 U/step. Step angle of the cheap motor is
   assumed (20 steps/rev) - confirm with the seller. See `parts.md` table 1.3.
4. **Reservoir slot:** the carriage tongue passes a 1.6 mm slot in the reservoir wall; the slot is an extra leak
   path to the dry side only (the fluid is sealed by the two lip rings), but the reservoir is **not** a closed
   volume on the dry side - the plunger rear faces the pump interior.
5. **Height 8.5 mm** unchanged (see spec); top fillet 1.0 mm.
6. **Closure bands** are thin (0.4/0.45 mm) because the 0.9 mm wall has to carry tongue + skirt + gasket seat;
   far-end (X = L) clamping relies on the lap and the lid stiffness - screws are only at the dry end and at the coil side.
   If the lid bows at the far end, add two snap hooks in Rev B or move screw C to the far end after the coil is
   made a PCB-trace coil.
7. **Hex nut orientation, bushing availability, motor ratio/step angle and gearbox output-shaft diameter** are
   unverified (see `parts.md` status column).
8. **No watertightness claim:** PA12 MJF is porous; the housing is for splash-level tests at best (gasket + SLA).

## Open points

* Cannula geometry is placeholder (H, K, L not wired into the geometry); latch stiffness/tooth height unverified.
* Screw thread, gear teeth, spring contacts are not modelled (smooth cylinders).
* No friction/leak/force analysis; plunger seal interference (0.15 mm per side) is unverified.
* Piezo/button/coil keep-outs are mechanical envelopes only - electronics must confirm components fit.
