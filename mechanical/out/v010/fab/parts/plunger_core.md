**Tolerances / features:** +-0.2 mm (vendor) on a 4.5 x 25.5 mm stadium (core clearance 0.1 per side in the CAD: the vendor +-0.2 is absorbed by the seal bead (squeeze 0.03 mm) and the PTFE-tape recess).

**Post-processing:** Sand or hone the core flanges to the bore (wrapped dowel, 1500 grit) so that it slides without the seal; optional 0.1 mm PTFE tape wrap (tape recess is designed in). Screw socket d2.5 x 2.0 and the 0.3 mm tab: do not sand the tab.

**Notes:** Pushed only (pressure fill, spec 3c); anti-rotation of the screw = hacksaw slot 0.4 on the screw tip engaging the tab. WATER ONLY.

JLC3DP vendor tolerances: SLA +-0.2 mm (holes shrink, +-0.3), MJF +-0.3 mm (holes shrink, +-0.3); the CAD clearances already assume them (dfm_rules.json).
