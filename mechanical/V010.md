# OpenPatch v0.1.0 - the integrated pod (mechanical variant `v010`, 2026-11-19)

One coherent model of every decision up to 2026-11-19 (spec 2a-2d, 3a-3e, 4b, 4c, 7a, 8a, the 2026-11-19 review and the owner's "exactly one rod" requirement). It replaces the earlier tail layout and the
zero-tail stack layout (`op_v010.py`, `op_v010s.py`, `op_cradle.py` are superseded; they stay in git history and are not used).

```bash
cd mechanical
python3 generate.py --variant v010                 # OP-100 + OP-200 -> out/v010/ (clean checkout: no cached .sc / STEP needed)
python3 check.py --variant v010                    # geometry, interference, fluid boundary, H14, one rod, electronics, walls; TARGET lines for the length goals
python3 check.py --variant v010 --assembly         # + insertion paths, tool/finger access, DFM with the vendor rules, fit classes
python3 fab.py --variant v010                      # out/v010/fab/ with a README per part (fab/parts/)
python3 sim/run_all.py                             # sim/REPORT-v010.md
python3 studies/pod_levers.py                      # the length levers below
```

## 1. Result (honest)

| size | built L x W x H | spec 2b target | gap | stroke | OP check | what limits the length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OP-100 | **58.24 x 32.0 x 8.4** | <= 45 | **+13.2** | 8.62 mm (100 U) | geometry PASS | motor stack in the rear zone (20.6 mm) + frozen dock tail 21.2 |
| OP-200 | **66.85 x 32.0 x 8.4** | <= 54 | **+12.9** | 17.23 mm (200 U) | geometry PASS | same rear zone; reservoir 25.2 mm; frozen dock tail 21.2 |

**The length targets are NOT reached and cannot be reached by this architecture.** `check.py` prints `TARGET MISS` with the gap (it does not fail the geometry check; `--strict` makes it fatal).
The reasons, with numbers (`studies/pod_levers.py`, pure arithmetic of `op_pod.layout`):

1. The owner's requirement (one rod only: no idler, no side rod, no encoder lane) forces the motor to sit **beside the screw tail in the rear zone**, because the gear pair z16:z20 has a fixed centre distance
   of 5.51 mm and the gears can be at most 6.6 mm in diameter (cavity height). The NFP-608 stack is 17.6 mm, so the nut gear plane is at x 18.7 and the reservoir starts at x 20.6 for **both** sizes.
   The old idler variant (motor beside the *reservoir*) saves the 9 mm but needs the second gear axis; with a 22 x 4.7 bore it gives 52.2 / 72.4 mm (OP-100 / OP-200), i.e. it is not better for OP-200 either.
2. The coaxial architecture costs one stroke of dry screw tail (`2 s + const`): OP-200 (2 ml) is >= 58 mm even with every lever below (full width bore 28 x 4.7: s = 15.8 mm).
3. The CNC wall of 1.0 mm (JLCCNC minimum) with the 8.4 mm height limits the bore height to 4.7 mm (5.0 mm would need 8.8 mm overall).

| lever set (all changes are recorded, none is hidden) | OP-100 L | OP-200 L |
|---|---|---|
| **built baseline**: NFP-608 rear zone, bore 25.7 x 4.7, frozen dock tail 21.2 | **58.2** | **66.9** |
| + Zhaowei d3.4 motor (envelope 14.1 mm stack; torque/shaft unverified) | 54.7 | 66.4 |
| + dock tail 21.2 -> 19.0 (decision: fill septum off the insert footprint, datum moves 2.2 mm) | 56.0 | 64.7 |
| + dock tail 21.2 -> 17.2 (decision, f = 4 of the drive study) | 54.2 | 62.9 |
| + no lid-screw lane, bore 28.0 wide (decision: lid fixed by snap hooks) | 57.5 | 65.4 |
| Zhaowei + tail 17.2 + bore 28.0 | 50.0 | 59.4 |
| **all levers** (+ end wall 1.5, dry strip 0.5) | **49.0** | **58.4** |
| reference only, forbidden by the owner: idler + side pin, bore 22.0 | 52.2 | 72.4 |

The **best reachable length is about 49 mm (OP-100) and 58 mm (OP-200)**, 4 mm over the targets for both; it needs the Zhaowei motor (not yet measured), a decision on the frozen dock datum and a lid fixed without
side screws. The dock datum was **not** moved in the built model (no silent change of a frozen interface); the decision is open (STATUS).

## 3. Decisions taken in the model (all recorded here, to be copied into `docs/STATUS.md` by the coordinator)

1. `reservoir_frozen_v010` (bore 25.7 x 4.7, wall 1.0, end wall 2.0, port d2.0 at y = dock axis, outer 27.7 x 6.7) replaces `reservoir_frozen` for v0.1.0; the bench block is unchanged.
2. Bore height 4.7 instead of 5.0 so that a 1.0 mm CNC wall fits the 8.4 mm height.
3. Fill septum d3.2 instead of d4.0 (fluid sandwich walls), needle bore d0.9 x 2.6 (vendor hole depth rule, <= 3 x d).
4. Screw insertion hole d2.5 in the rear wall (plugged with a silicone stopper): the screw is longer than the rear zone (OP-200: 23 mm), so it cannot be inserted from inside.
5. Lid fixed by 6 M1.2 screws (5 along the -Y lane, 1 rear +Y, none beside the reservoir): 1.0 mm wall reservoir 27.7 wide leaves only the 2.3 mm lane at -Y.
6. Dock datum **not** moved in x (frozen), see the lever table. **The z interface of the dock insert was changed for the DFM hard floors:** ledge 0.45 -> 0.50 and a 0.4 mm floor pad in the dock zone (floor 1.3), so the flange slot is 0.82 mm and the
   insert flange 0.73 mm (MJF slot floor 0.8, SLA wall floor 0.4); the v0.1.0 insert set (`out/v010/fab/dock-inserts`) is NOT interchangeable with the bench pod's inserts. To be recorded in `docs/STATUS.md`.
7. Flat-joint closure (gasket on the wall top, lid from the gasket plane, no lap tongue / skirt): vendor tolerances cannot hold a 0.4 + 0.1 + 0.4 lap in a 0.9 mm wall.
8. Fluid sandwich: O-ring 2.0 x 0.7 (groove depth 0.55), cap block top 6.8 (was 6.4), gasket recess 1.0 .. 3.9, one clamp screw (M1.2, no counterbore, pocket in the pod lid over its head).
9. Fill-scale engraving and the lid recess narrowed to the reservoir top; the lid is fixed by 6 screws (5 along the -Y lane + 1 rear +Y); the +Y long edge has no screw beside the reservoir (open: snap hooks).

### 4.2 `check.py --variant v010 --assembly` (`out/v010/assembly-check/REPORT.md`, OP-100 full, OP-200 paths / access / fits)

* **0 blocking findings.** 32 build steps, every insertion path free (the reservoir module slides 1.2 mm over the nut, the screw enters from the rear through the d2.5 hole, spine / detents are flexible parts), tweezer and
  screwdriver access OK for every small part and all 6 lid screws + the clamp screw.
* **DFM (vendor hard floors, `dfm_rules.json`): no part below a hard floor** (bottom MJF, clear lid SLA, end cap, end cap lid, plunger core, 12 dock inserts, mould, CNC reservoirs); only vendor-recommended
  wall / hole warnings remain (MJF wall 0.9 vs recommended 1.45; channels d0.5-0.9 below the SLA vendor minimum 1.0 are post-cleared with a wire). Needed design changes found by the checker and made: flat joint instead of a 0.4 mm lap
  tongue, floor pad + 0.5 mm ledge in the dock zone, fill septum d3.2, gasket recess 1.0 .. 3.9 open at the sides, O-ring 2.0 x 0.7 in a 0.55 deep groove (cap block top 6.8), screw blocks flush to the walls, spigot half pins
  y-limited, pilots <= 2.9 deep, ID strokes 0.5 mm.
* **Fits with the vendor tolerances (JLC3DP SLA +-0.2, MJF +-0.3, holes shrink):** bush in the bearing block, plunger core in the CNC bore, spigot in the port, hub in its seat, M1.2 pilots fail as printed and have a documented
  post-process in `ASSEMBLY.md` (ream d3.05, sand the core 1500 grit, sand the spigot to d1.95, ream the hub seat 2.0, drill d1.0 / d1.4); sleeve, gear, screw / bush / sleeve, ring magnet, dock magnet, lid screw clearance, board pin pass or are at risk
  only in the vendor scenario; snap arms SLA / MJF OK.
* Not checked: flexible parts (spine FPC, silicone), friction / press-in forces, the real component models (NFP STEP, gears, rod are envelopes).

## 5. Interface for the electronics agent

`out/v010/OP-xx.layout.json` (schema `openpatch-v010-layout/3`): pod size, z stack, MAIN board outline (pod and u frame) with locating pins, zones (dock tunnel 0.0 mm bottom height, cell wing, spine tongue), cell bay
and pads, SENSOR board + TMAG position and ring axis, USB-C geometry, motor pin positions and contact card, spine FPC, vent, piezo, drive constants. **Boards decided by the electronics agent**: radio ISP1807 8 x 8 x 1.0 on MAIN
(<= 400 mm2: the pod gives 344 mm2 incl. tongue), power parts on a separate POWER board (~572 mm2), SENSOR board with the TMAG and the two dock halls. **Volume gap:** only about 190 mm2 of full-height pocket area (rear
pocket 13 x 14 mm beside the USB-C and the SENSOR board; 5 x 20 mm strip beside the motor) exists for the POWER board: a 572 mm2 board does not fit in this pod; it needs either stacking above the SENSOR board
(z 4.2 .. 7.4, 3 mm) at ~120 mm2 or the MAIN wing (which the cell occupies below), i.e. **the POWER board must shrink to about 250 mm2 or the pod grows by about 12 mm**.

## 6. Open items

1. Length targets (section 1): decisions on the frozen dock datum / Zhaowei motor / lid fixation; best reachable 49 / 58 mm.
2. Thrust margin >= 2 at 160 mA is not met (`sim/REPORT-v010.md` 1.3: 1.14 against 40 kPa back-pressure on the wide bore); it needs a 12:20 pair (8.2 N, margin 1.5 at 160 mA, U/step 0.0046) or an occlusion criterion of <= 20 kPa.
3. Ring magnet availability (custom d5 x d2 x 1.5 diametric), TMAG A2 range, firmware angle calibration.
4. FPC spine and motor contact card: electronics agent (pinout, ZIF vs spring contacts, 14+ lines in 3.6 mm).
5. Real component models (NFP STEP, gears, rod) were not inserted into the v010 assembly (envelopes only); the interference check covers the envelope bodies.
6. Cannula head dimensions (dome 5.0 mm) are still TODO-MEASURE; the board stack depends on them (0.45 mm over the dome).
7. Dock insert fit, snap arms in clear/tough SLA (strain 1.9-2.1 %, limit 2 %), vendor DFM of the CNC reservoir (OP-200 pocket depth 23 mm = 4.9 x tool).
