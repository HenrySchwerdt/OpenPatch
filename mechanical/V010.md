# OpenPatch v0.1.0 - integrated pod, mechanical variant `v010` (2026-11-19)

The bench pod (frozen reservoir interface, drive, end cap, modular dock insert on the frozen interface) plus the electronics: integrated board, Li-Po cell, buzzer, button,
charge pads behind a gasketed lid window, pressure vent. **Additive**: `op_v010.py`, `v010_gen.py`, `v010_check.py`, `v010_fab.py`, `op_cradle.py`, `sim/free_volume.py`, the block
`params.json -> v010`, and small hooks (`p["v010_active"]`) in `generate.py`, `check.py`, `fab.py`, `op_layout.py` (extra lid screws) and `op_shell.py` (no FFC slot, lead groove ends at the
board connector, no fill window). `reservoir_frozen` and `dock_interface_frozen` are untouched; the bench variant still passes (`check.py --variant bench`, OP-100).

```bash
cd mechanical
python3 generate.py --variant v010            # OP-100/200 -> out/v010/ (renders for OP-100 only; --snapshot-sizes OP-100 OP-200 for more); builds missing rod STEPs automatically
python3 check.py --variant v010               # all sizes + cradle
python3 fab.py --variant v010                 # out/v010/fab/
python3 sim/free_volume.py out/bench/OP-100.sc   # free-volume map (section 8)
```

## 2. Why the tail is beside the dock, not behind it (finding that changes the architecture estimate)

The Insight Flex dome slides onto the pod **from the +X end** (bench `dock`, open mouth in the end wall, insert `tail_u` 21.2 frozen). A tail *behind* the dock would close the mouth. So:

* the frozen insert keeps its bench length (same printed part for bench and v0.1.0), the housing continues the tunnel to the new end wall (floor window cut by the existing interface, `t_dwin`);
* the tunnel (y 5.0 .. 17.4 at the arms, dome path y 5.0 .. 16.9) must stay free below the board (board bottom z 5.3, dome top 5.0, insert top 5.2): **no bottom-side part there except the dock hall pocket**;
* all tall bottom-side parts (E73 2.25, PicoBlade 3.4, cell 3.2 + foam) therefore sit in the **strip beside the tunnel**, y >= 17.9 (arms) / 16.7 (dome only): a 12-13 mm wide, ~0.9 .. 5.3 high strip. They are lined up along X:
  `[TMAG zone, u 6.15] [PicoBlade plug+connector u 6.2 .. 13.4] [cell bay u 14.2 .. 27.7] [E73 u 28.6 .. 46.6] [end gap 0.8]` -> tail 47.5 mm vs bench 20.3 mm.
* The board covers the strip, the wing over the spindle sensors (TMAG/DRV positions unchanged vs magnet) and, for u >= 12, the full width over the tunnel (top-side parts <= 1.3 mm, dock hall pocket at the unchanged dock position, board 0.1 mm above the insert).

## 4. Battery choice (documented size class)

two 301015/301020 samples; thickness must be measured (< 3.3 mm).

## 5. Cradle (`op_cradle.py`, `out/v010/cradle/`, fab copies in `out/v010/fab/cradle/`)

One printed cradle per size (pad x depends on x_r1): pod laid **lid down** into a tray (rim 1.6 x 4 mm, 0.3 mm clearance), two **P75-E2-class spring test probes** (tube d1.02, length 16.5 mm, stroke ~2 mm;
AliExpress/LCSC "P75-E2", VERIFY with calipers) pressed into d1.08 bores, tips 2.35 mm below the lid face = pad plane, **1.15 mm compression**; a 2 mm bar on two M2 x 8 screws holds the pod down. Pin tails (solder cups) at the bottom: wire them
to a USB-C 5 V breakout (the cradle is a bench fixture, not the pod; a screw-clamp lug variant is possible). No magnets: the pod has no iron. `check_cradle` verifies bore/pin/window alignment by interference (pins enter the window, no contact with the lid).

## 7. Assembly order (no soldering iron on the pod)

1. Boards: PCBA from the assembler with E73, PicoBlade, piezo, tact, TMAG, DRV5032s, spring contacts, interlock/driver parts (reflow only).
2. Bottom shell: foam tape in the bay, cell onto the foam (PCM pads up, -X end), check the ribs; drive, reservoir, end cap, dock insert as in the bench pod (`ASSEMBLY.md`).
3. Motor lead: plug into the board connector **before** lowering the board (plug enters from -X), lay the lead flat in the floor groove between the strain-relief posts.
4. Board onto the pins/post (spring contacts land on the cell pads), M1.2 board screw.
5. Charge-window gasket onto the board (around the pads), main gasket, clear lid (ePTFE vent disc, PU buzzer film glued into the recesses first), 6 x M1.2 x 6.
6. Tests in the order of ARCHITECTURE 8: bench splash test, charge cycle on the cradle, then the drive tests. Water only.
